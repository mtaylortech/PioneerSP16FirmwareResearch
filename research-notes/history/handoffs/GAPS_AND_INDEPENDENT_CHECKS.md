## Reproduction rules for the independent reviewer

Use original `.UPD` files and a fresh isolated output/workspace. Identify code, inputs, dependencies and commands **before** a new run. Keep native final output, per-thread exceptions, stdout/stderr, supervisor/process-tree exit codes and wall times. Label the new run's date and identity. Compare it against prior artifacts as a reproduction, never as proof that a missing historical run occurred.

Inspect reproduction instructions before executing them: decoder rebuild and older documentation validators can overwrite outputs/reports, and vendor setup/update/deploy scripts contain destructive commands. The reproduction appendix supplies isolation guidance and records dependencies/known obstacles. Runtime/hardware/firmware-write procedures are outside this documentation authorization; no such command was executed here.

The evidence that most deserves independent challenge is the request-buffer interpretation, the amount/completion of original startup work, the effects of substituted readiness/TLS/device/audio behavior, source-to-shipped boot correspondence, and full updater acceptance/authenticity. Preserving these uncertainties is part of the handoff's purpose.
