# Stage 5 — Approval recommendation

Review date: October 9, 2026, America/Chicago.

**Final advisory verdict: APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS.** Only the human project owner may authorize adoption of the roadmap. This recommendation grants no execution, implementation, hardware, flashing or communication authority.

The [audit] `Review/05-ROADMAP-AUDIT.md` (evidence not published) establishes six Major findings and two Minor findings, with no Critical finding established. The [correction document] `Review/05-REQUIRED-CORRECTIONS.md` (evidence not published) gives specific revisions and closure criteria. This verdict applies to the identified Stage 4 submission, not to an unreviewed later revision.

## Why conditional approval is defensible

The roadmap redirects the project toward meaningful functionality on a physical instrument. It preserves verified extraction/UI work while keeping kernel boot, real audio, changed functionality, deployment and recovery unproved. It requires a selected feature, actual access/execution evidence and affected-interface feasibility before broad implementation. It places recovery before persistent exposure and treats D/E/F as alternatives justified by requirements rather than an inevitable sequence. [destination and baseline] `Review/04-MASTER-ROADMAP.md:7` (evidence not published), [persistent ordering] `Review/04-DEPENDENCY-MAP.md:94` (evidence not published).

That is the most defensible **planning direction supported by the reviewed evidence**. The evidence does not establish a unique fastest route, a viable deployment mechanism or a functioning modified/replacement OS. The retained-platform preference remains a hypothesis; the product, unit and mechanism evidence may change it. [Stage 2 objective/deployment findings] `Review/02-INDEPENDENT-AUDIT.md:33` (evidence not published), [architecture alternatives] `Review/03-STRATEGIC-OPTIONS.md:62` (evidence not published).

Unconditional approval is inappropriate because several gates can demand evidence before it can be acquired, certify less than their intended capability, or impose unselected persistence. Wholesale rejection is unwarranted because these are correctable contracts, not demonstrated architectural impossibility. No actual unsafe experiment was performed or authorized by the proposal.

## Conditions of adoption

The human owner should require a consistent revised proposal and review of these six conditions. If adopting a staged plan while later conditions remain unresolved, the affected gates must remain explicitly withheld; adoption must not imply their clearance.

| Condition | Required disposition |
|---|---|
| RC01 — M02/M03 readiness | Split preservation/capture readiness from independent reference reproduction; align all prerequisite representations before scheduling these gates. |
| RC02 — Resource admission | Identify measured stock capacity, bounded candidate costs and unresolved integration; require integrated target qualification before performance/functionality claims. |
| RC03 — Active hardware safety | Add applicable independent intervention and bounded physical-output conditions before active on-unit trials, including volatile ones. |
| RC04 — Persistent candidate binding | Bind every persistent candidate/installer/unit to the qualified write and recovery envelope before exposure; distinguish unchanged-route acceptance from encryption equality. |
| RC05 — Delivery-path qualification | Permit equivalent qualification for an expressly non-installed product, or record the owner's actual requirement for persistence. |
| RC06 — Modification mechanism | Prove or explicitly gate the selected app-patch/interposition mechanism before substantial feature implementation; unrelated program execution is insufficient. |

RC07's discriminating second-feature test and RC08's bounded feasibility investment are worthwhile improvements that do not block adoption. Conditions can be declared inapplicable only through an explicit product/route decision that removes their exposure. Missing evidence is not proof of inapplicability.

Closing these corrections establishes a usable plan. It does not close M01's product choice, unit availability, executable access, physical interfaces, source/build correspondence or recovery. Those remain technical/product gates requiring separate evidence and human-authorized scopes.

## Claim boundaries after adoption

A legal stock parameter edit proves a mechanism, not new functionality. A stand-alone identity program proves execution, not attachment to proprietary processing. M07 earns bounded software behavior; M08 earns the selected new physical feature; installation/lifecycle and supported-release gates earn their larger claims. A userspace feature cannot inherit the title of replacement OS. If a complete replacement system is a retained owner requirement, its selected BE/BF substrate and agreed instrument-function suite still have to pass. [M07/M08] `Review/04-MASTER-ROADMAP.md:183` (evidence not published), [M12 replacement boundary] `Review/04-MASTER-ROADMAP.md:254` (evidence not published).

Stop or replan when no suitable unit/route exists, selected mechanism feasibility fails, intervention is unavailable, write/recovery scope is unbounded, required state/output or regressions fail, or integrated target costs exceed the declared envelope. Preserve narrower valid results and reject only the disproved route; do not turn failure into automatic permission for a rewrite.

The review is complete. Only the three Stage 5 review documents were created. The roadmap and historical evidence remain unchanged. No subagent, implementation, runtime experiment, hardware action or message dispatch follows from this recommendation.
