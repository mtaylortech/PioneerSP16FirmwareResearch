# Stage 1 — Evidence gaps and conflicting records

Review date: October 9, 2026, America/Chicago. Companion [inventory] `Review/01-PROJECT-INVENTORY.md` (evidence not published) describes coverage and active work; [baseline] `Review/01-TECHNICAL-BASELINE.md` (evidence not published) supplies B01–B30 findings and S01–S28 evidence locations. This is a record of missing evidence and review limits, not a prioritized roadmap, experiment authorization, or implementation prompt.

## Main conclusion

The extraction and original-application/UI milestones have substantially better evidence than the modified/replacement-OS objective. The outstanding gap is between a useful software research environment and demonstrated instrument functionality or deployment. No examined record closes that gap.

Several old negative results have been superseded at their narrow technical boundaries. Several historical evidence defects remain real even though newer tooling avoids them. Neither a clean later run nor a successful static inspection turns an earlier missing final or failed observer into a success.

## Records and access limitations

1. **No atomic review snapshot.** A different chat is modifying archival/storage records. Directory statistics and archive status can change after their observed checkpoint. These documents preserve claims from inspected artifacts rather than certify the entire live tree's final storage state.
2. **Primary artifacts exist outside Git.** Ignored raw logs, native finals, captures, originals, rootfs and databases are available locally but missing from a fresh checkout. One late initial commit cannot establish earlier execution chronology or recover ignored evidence.
3. **Unavailable versus unexamined is distinguished.** Legacy path is absent; official product firmware URL returned403; vendor internal SVN access/credentials are unestablished. Compressed archive restores, every earlier chat turn and exhaustive vendor source were not performed; they are coverage limitations, not claimed permission denials.
4. **Conversation search is supplementary.** Both pages of archived Codex metadata were inspected; selected extraction/research conversations were read. Recent unarchived listing is capped at50, and some read responses truncate content or have older-page cursors. Complete raw historical conversations are not certified by this review. Raw filesystem evidence takes precedence over agent summaries.
5. **Manual/source provenance remains qualified.** Local service manual is a held document; hardware claims are documentary. Public source page availability confirms its listing, not exact equality to the held archives. No new publisher firmware/source comparison was made.
6. **Failed assertion receipts are incomplete.** Original Send assertion lacks actual operands; roster failure lacks stage/expected frame detail. Later static/readback evidence does not recover those values at the failed instant.
7. **Selected controls are version-specific.** Counts such as13,56,61,70,140 or141 mean saved checks for particular sources/scopes. Synthetic effects or copied exports do not independently validate original application behavior. No project tests or verifiers were run in Stage1.
8. **Independent cleanup is bounded evidence.** Recent direct leaders/watchdog were reaped and postflight passes recorded. Earlier coverage gaps/PermissionError remain. Second-resolution start identity and unrecorded descendants that lose ancestry between samples limit a universal no-survivor assertion. Stage1 did not launch a fresh process census.
9. **No hidden positive reconstruction.** Excluded async findings, absent native finals, unrecorded failures/successes and missing earlier instruction receipts remain missing. A stopped or paused task has not been silently resumed.

## Which findings can be relied on now

Both held releases' identity/integrity, accessible decoded baseline, original app static bytes, mapped startup completion, owned HOME/Mixer output, tagged Track/Mixer responses and narrow Send snapshots have inspectable evidence. Stage1 independently reproduced their selected file/CRC/byte/pixel/snapshot readbacks. Full historical decryption/path audits and live behavior have retained reproduction assets but were not replayed here.

General performance, complete instrument usability, Send mutation, full deferred application, DSP/audio correctness, source builds, repack acceptance, recovery and a new operating system remain unsupported as completed capabilities. They are uncertainties recorded for later stages; this stage proposes no new sequence, estimates, staffing or implementation tasks.
