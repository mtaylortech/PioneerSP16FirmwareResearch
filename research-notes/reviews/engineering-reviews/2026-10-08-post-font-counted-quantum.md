# Post-font counted quantum: engineering review and next experiments

Review date: October 8, 2026, America/Chicago. Main cycle: `post-font-counted-quantum-20261008-repeat`; preceding inconclusive pair: `post-font-counted-quantum-20261008`.

**Assessment:** the repeat demonstrated useful, partial original resource advancement. Retain the 313,370-instruction post-font condition for further bounded resource work. The primary selected enclosing-constructor milestone was not reached, full startup remains unconfirmed, and the display remains black. Scheduling overhead as the dominant cause, low-latency worker fairness, and general reliability are not established.

This review read the implementation, raw events, native finals, recorded identities, supervision and postflight. A separate offline calculation reconstructed the common post-font window and ordered prefixes, checked recorded layout frame relationships, checked current harness hashes against launch records, and decoded all six XWD captures. Original ELF inspection identified another enclosing boundary. These are cross-checks of retained evidence, **not independent guest replications or independent validation of the emulator/observer implementation**. No firmware guest, new scheduling trial, or proposed next experiment was launched. ## 1. Original objectives and expectations

The documented question was whether increasing only eligible identified-main calls from count 31,337 to 313,370 after validated font epilogue `0x706d04` would advance original resource construction. Early initialization/font scheduling, workers, explicit execution settings, syscall stops, TLS, cache-transition behavior, guards and originals were to remain unchanged. Scheduling overhead was explicitly a hypothesis.

Primary success was an earlier matched `0x4007a4 → 0x4013ac → 0x324388` enclosing return, or treatment reaching it while control did not within the comparable post-font window. Greater validated resource-prefix progress without that return was defined as partial success. Regression, required-worker starvation or unexplained faults rejected treatment; an invalid arm required an inconclusive comparison. Font completion times and full observation bounds were to be reported separately, with comparison restricted to the shorter post-font interval.

Those criteria were clear enough to evaluate. Two expectations were **not** established before execution: a numerical expected speedup and proof that the selected constructor must occur on this particular startup route. The review's alternative enclosing boundary is a finding developed afterward, not an original prediction. ## 4. What went correctly

The common post-font time window prevented early-phase timing from masquerading as resource acceleration. Identical sparse observations, stored return timestamps and ordered layout paths supplied evidence beyond callback totals. The byte/caller/SP matcher appropriately refused to substitute a pop for a completed constructor. Completed-question tracing was retired identically, and synchronous exception capture remained intact.

Recorded launch checks passed 27 app/ELF identities per arm, and current harness hashes match the recorded launch hashes. The original app hash used in static review matches the held identity. This review did not independently prove every installed-runtime/font file's preservation or rerun cleanup controls; their recorded checks and unchanged preparation are the evidence. Both native postflights support the owned cleanup records. Preserve the tested count-selection code, fresh seed, exception capture, private display, sparse matched frames, timestamps and unchanged passing supervisor controls. Routine cleanup/doc generation supports validity but is not a startup milestone. [Control supervision] `../experiments/post-font-counted-quantum-20261008-repeat/control/attempt-supervision.json` (evidence not published), [postflight] `../experiments/post-font-counted-quantum-20261008-repeat/control/endpoint-postflight-initial.json` (evidence not published), [treatment supervision] `../experiments/post-font-counted-quantum-20261008-repeat/treatment/attempt-supervision.json` (evidence not published), [postflight] `../experiments/post-font-counted-quantum-20261008-repeat/treatment/endpoint-postflight-initial.json` (evidence not published).

No independently reproducible guest gain has yet been demonstrated: there was one valid pair. The earlier inconclusive pair cannot serve as replication.

## 5. What failed and why

The primary enclosing-constructor and usable-UI objectives failed to materialize within the bound. No runtime exception was recorded in this repeat; bounded expiry alone is not evidence of a deadlock. At the final common-window samples, control remained at main PC `0x73f63c` with a resource frame pending; treatment had advanced to `0x66d710`, with no resource-helper frame pending in its final summary. An empty helper stack does not mean global resource construction completed: main can be between helper calls.

Competing explanations for the unentered target are unfinished preceding work, a different active construction route, conditional/later use of that view, or observations missing a relevant caller/state. The known bytes and installed hooks support that no target entry was observed, but not why. Sparse active enclosing-frame observation can distinguish preceding work from a wrong milestone assumption. No claim of a stall at `0x4007a4` is justified.

The control helper's `NameError` was an implementation error in introspection, not an environmental guest failure; the correction and passing retry are retained. Prior font-lock waits and misaligned-pointer faults remain unexplained. Retiring diagnostics may change timed interleaving; the healthy repeat does not establish that retirement fixed those failures.

## 6. What surprised us

Both font returns were within 0.107s, despite the prior pair's pre-activation font stall. This makes the repeat useful for performance comparison but does not explain the earlier failure. Treatment reached the shared 281-return prefix much sooner, yet still never entered the selected constructor. That suggests the original sequence/choice of enclosing targets deserves scrutiny; it does not demonstrate an alternate architecture.

Workers were sampled much less frequently under treatment while continuing to receive slices. That is expected from larger main slices and is a fairness limitation to investigate if latency becomes relevant, not itself proof of starvation. The current evidence supports progress more clearly than mechanism or responsiveness.

## 7. Advancement toward the ultimate goal

This cycle moved original resource construction farther along a validated common prefix within the existing budget. It supplies a concrete better condition for reaching later startup boundaries and removes the need to spend the next run simply asking whether count 313,370 can help at all.

It did not advance the selected enclosing-constructor, completed-startup or usable-UI milestones. It also did not exceed the historical best 584-return total from a different five-minute experiment; those totals have different bounds/observations and are not a performance comparison. No new hardware, flashing, recovery or replacement-feature capability was validated.

Additional generic callback inventories, audio totals, broad preservation audits or unchanged CSV pointer observations would have little practical value for this outcome. The critical uncertainty is the active enclosing construction route and its return into later application/drawing state. Earlier parent traversal remains completed work.

## 8. Highest-value proposed experiments

These are plans only. Each future guest needs a fresh same-seed writable copy, validated new hook bytes, private vacant endpoints/credentials, retained guards, synchronous exceptions and independent finite supervision. ### 1. Match the enclosing routine actually reached by treatment — High; recommended next

- **Objective:** determine whether the observed resource-containing routine returns, and what main continuation follows it.
- **Hypothesis:** the routine at `0x40d6e8` is an active enclosing construction stage whose matched return is a better immediate boundary than the currently unentered target. Its role in full startup remains tentative.
- **Procedure:** retain the tested 313,370 post-font condition and unchanged early/fonts scheduling. In a fresh harness add identical read-only entry `0x40d6e8`, pop `0x40df28`, and caller `0x322b40` observations; validate original prologue, SP adjustment, BL at `0x322b3c` and caller bytes. Preserve `0x4007a4` observations. Run one future-authorized 180-second guest with 185/188/190 limits. Record the continuation after the matched return and sparse main state.
- **Expected:** entry, saved caller at pop SP+32, pop SP = entry SP−36, restored entry SP at caller return, then genuine subsequent main execution.
- **Alternatives:** a pending matched frame identifies unfinished enclosing work; a completed frame with target still absent redirects attention to later caller/state rather than the selected constructor; another caller requires byte-validated learning and honest mismatch reporting.
- **Success:** a correctly matched enclosing return plus subsequent execution. A specific pending frame/local boundary is a useful diagnostic partial result, not completion. Full startup/UI still require separate evidence.
- **Resources/risk:** existing ELF/unwind information, observer framework/runtime/seed/display. Only instrumentation/guest-state risk; observation can perturb timed interleaving. Preserve completed evidence and do not bypass original instructions.

### 2. Replicate the finite-count gain with reversed arm order — Medium

- **Objective:** determine whether the useful resource-prefix gain survives a second valid pair and host/order variability.
- **Hypothesis:** treatment extends the matching prefix at a common post-font endpoint again.
- **Procedure:** future two-arm 180/185/188/190 comparison, treatment first, identical observations and independent same-seed copies. Keep the scheduling change exclusively post-font; reuse count controls. Report constructor returns and shorter post-font window, not full-run totals alone.
- **Expected:** consistent prefix extension without new post-font faults or sampled loss of required worker service.
- **Alternatives:** equal/regressed progress weakens retention; divergent paths require investigation; an early-font failure makes the pair inconclusive, not automatically a treatment regression.
- **Success:** repeatable original prefix or enclosing-boundary advancement under comparable windows. A second healthy pair still does not establish general reliability.
- **Resources/risk:** unchanged harness/supervisor, roughly two guest bounds. Main risk is spending a cycle validating speed rather than advancing the startup boundary; perform after the route observation unless retention becomes uncertain.

### 3. Separate cooperative early/font scheduling comparison — Medium; reliability priority

- **Objective:** determine whether font progress can survive removal of asynchronous timed stops.
- **Hypothesis:** the existing syscall-boundary candidate can reach the original font epilogue and resource phase without the native timer-replay mechanism.
- **Procedure:** separate future comparison of supported timed early/fonts versus cooperative early/fonts; restore the same 313,370 counted post-font condition in both. Validate the new phase limiter and original dispatcher/TLS/worker semantics before guests. Use 180/185/188/190 bounds and compare matched font/resource boundaries.
- **Expected:** all five BDFs/17 scan faces and matched font return with continued worker service.
- **Alternatives:** long CPU-only stretches, wait states, major regression or faults reject the candidate; a healthy run does not prove historical corruption provenance.
- **Success:** matched original font completion and useful post-font progress within the bound, preserved dispatcher/guards, no unexplained fault or sampled starvation.
- **Resources/risk:** [existing candidate] `../experiments/atomic-startup-20261008-session/syscall_boundary_quantum.py` (evidence not published), focused native controls and supervision. Cooperative duration is advisory, so independent shutdown is essential. This is not part of the quantum-retention decision already tested.

### 4. Follow completed construction into original drawable output — Medium, conditional

- **Objective:** identify the transition from relevant enclosing construction completion to mapped, drawn and responsive original UI.
- **Hypothesis:** a later main/display boundary, rather than additional helper count alone, explains the black output.
- **Procedure:** after the relevant enclosing return is demonstrated, correlate sparse main continuation with existing original X11 map/draw calls, private server drawable ownership and captures. Test one bounded local input only after an original drawable is identified; keep observation scope private.
- **Expected:** an owned drawable with actual original nonblack pixels and an attributable response.
- **Alternatives:** no draw activity redirects to later main state; draw activity with black captures requires drawable/protocol/content inspection; unfinished construction means the prerequisite was not met.
- **Success:** original drawable output plus observable input response. A child window, map call or changed root alone is insufficient.
- **Resources/risk:** existing authenticated display/capture tools and a separately authorized bounded guest. No physical device risk; avoid mistaking other display pixels or injected output for original rendering.

## 9. Single recommended next action

Run experiment 1 after it is authorized: **match `0x40d6e8 → 0x40df28 → 0x322b40` and follow its continuation using retained treatment.** The existing treatment's resource return at `0x40dee4` ties this boundary directly to observed work. Original ELF instructions establish the candidate frame layout and direct call; its live enclosing return remains unmeasured.

This takes priority over another speed comparison because it converts demonstrated helper progress into an actual enclosing boundary and tests the assumption that the previously selected view belongs on the current route. It enables a decision between unfinished construction, later application-state work and drawable investigation.

Executable plan: prepare one fresh copy from the recorded seed; add/validate only the three enclosing points and minimal caller continuation, with saved-return/restored-SP controls; retain the tested phase/count/worker/guard conditions; launch one bounded 180-second guest under independent 185/188/190 supervision; save raw events/final/cleanup; determine matched return and subsequent main stage. Preserve a pending frame, mismatch, exception or absent final as such. If the enclosing return is already complete but the selected target remains unentered, revise the route model. If fonts fail again, classify this as an invalid route-observation attempt and address early scheduling separately. Do not count more helper returns as the requested enclosing success.

**No proposed experiment was executed during this review.**

## 10. Final engineering assessment

- **What we expected:** larger counted post-font slices would advance resources, ideally a matched selected constructor return.
- **What actually happened:** a common-window 281→441 return/path-prefix extension; neither selected constructor entry nor usable UI appeared.
- **What succeeded:** matched partial resource progress, phase-normalized measurement, sparse frame controls and owned cleanup.
- **What failed:** the primary enclosing/UI milestone; initial control introspection failed and was corrected.
- **What surprised us:** a large shared-prefix timing gain with nearly identical fonts still did not reach the selected target.
- **What we learned:** 313,370 is worth retaining for bounded resource work; an observed active enclosing routine offers a better immediate boundary.
- **What remains uncertain:** performance mechanism/repeatability, worker latency, timed-startup reliability, construction route, full startup and original responsive drawing.
- **How much closer we are:** meaningful partial original resource progress; no new completed-startup/UI milestone.
- **The single most important thing to test next:** matched return and continuation of `0x40d6e8`, using its original pop and caller frame.
