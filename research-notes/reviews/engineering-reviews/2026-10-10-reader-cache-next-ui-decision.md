Ghidra exports are static preparation; executed cache branches are runtime results; six guard controls are synthetic validation. No performance improvement was measured.

Successful cases and ten reaped groups remain valid.

Original decoding and real owner invalidation remain normalization follow-ups. They do not currently explain the missing usable-UI interaction. Startup, Mixer navigation and passive ownership inspection also need no repetition as separate experiments.

Reuse unchanged controls; the [703.443-second cycle] `../experiments/reader-cache-fixture-20261010-082651/result.json` (evidence not published) is total elapsed work, not measured validation overhead or firmware speedup.
