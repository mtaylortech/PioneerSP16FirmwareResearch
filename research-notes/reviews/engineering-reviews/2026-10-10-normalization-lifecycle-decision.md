# Normalization editor lifecycle decision

October 10, 2026, America/Chicago. **Status 3: CURRENT APPROACH BLOCKED.** The inspected owner roster cannot yet serve as the editor boundary for same-original Normalize → Keep. The actual registered member, its constructed vtable, and its +8/+0xc methods have not been connected to editor producer closure, settlement or manager unload acknowledgment. Treating the roster preamble as that barrier would assume the central safety property.

Recommend deferring file replacement and changing the evidence method. A later, separately authorized sparse observation of the real registration and teardown route can discriminate this binding directly. If that route supplies no usable boundary, a candidate needs an explicit producer ledger, legal lifecycle serialization and acknowledgment association. The conditional specification below describes those proof obligations; it is **not an executable stock sequence or implementation-ready design**. This decision neither proves stock-wide absence nor identifies an observed stock race/use-after-free.

## Conditional protocol specification

All rows are requirements for a future candidate, not found stock primitives. **No row currently authorizes file replacement.** “Generation” below is candidate-owned accounting, not a field found in the firmware.

A transaction generation G must identify the exact O/E incarnation, selected original/storage identity and expected R incarnation, and retain an immutable selection/project/model snapshot. Request tickets must be created at the legal producer boundary **before task capture or any caller deferral**. Closure and ticket creation must have one serialized ordering: every racing request is either an accounted pre-closure producer or refused/deferred without an unaccounted raw E capture. Every producer capable of later matching the selected original must participate, including caller-held records. An unclassifiable target counts as potentially matching; unresolved aliases or bypasses require refusal. Merely adding a counter inside E's worker is insufficient.

| State / transition | Required owners and observable postcondition | Bound integration evidence / outstanding proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Eligible → Closed(G)** | Candidate controller and actual O lifecycle owner first obtain legal retention/serialization, freeze identity, then close new matching admission atomically with producer ticket creation. No destructive work. | O/E relation is bound in 09g. `0xb87c0`/`0xb8390` identify admission/capture, but a boundary covering all producers, selection changes and O/E teardown is **unbound**. If unavailable, refuse before starting. |
| **Closed → Settling(G)** | Account for all admitted tickets across caller records, E queues, execution and M load/result delivery. Permit those existing loads to reach their original result route. Producer tickets need a terminal reason exactly once, separately recording submission, execution, original load result, continuing outcome work and unload request/acknowledgment. A stock callback with no generation field could only be associated through a **proved unique, retained per-request context** at a legal integration boundary; no echoed generation is assumed. Concurrent stock unloads, reader reuse or listener sharing invalidate that inference unless distinguished. The adapter must preserve original listener behavior and result ordering; replacing the no-op callback in place is not itself a legal ownership mechanism.

**Selection/project/editor changes:** requests during closure must either be held by a proved owner-level serialization route or abort Keep and restore/revalidate. They cannot silently transfer G to a new editor or sample. R is read by the worker later, so accounting cannot assume the originally selected R remains current without that serialization. Project unload/reset and editor replacement are teardown cases, not ordinary selection updates.

**Busy/refusal:** use a defined candidate result before stock capture; do not invent a stock cancellation result. A deferred user operation may be replayed only after live identity is revalidated. Existing stock task/box records keep their original admission/result route. If producer coverage or legal retention cannot be established at eligibility, leave Normalize/Keep unavailable and preserve ordinary stock operation.

**Timeout/absent callback:** observation timeout is a failure to qualify, never completion. Continue only the proved stock settlement/lifetime route; keep Keep disabled and the original untouched. Do not fabricate flags, delete tasks, free raw captures or release retention while acknowledgment is missing. A safe recovery/quarantine capability is itself an outstanding prerequisite. Indefinitely freezing an editor or preventing required teardown would fail original-function preservation and cannot qualify the feature. Without a legal recovery/retention policy, refusal must occur before entry into this protocol.

## Discriminating validation plan — proposed, not run

First prepare deterministic model/adapter controls against the table above. They should expose ticket creation/retirement, closure ordering, E/R/listener ownership, immutable G/U identities, original result order and refusal/restoration outcomes. Enumerate both sides of closure/admission and callback/teardown races, without manufacturing stock completion. An adapter or mock pass proves only the specified model. It cannot establish real producer coverage, original object lifetime, release or firmware safety.

The smallest different original-evidence method is a separately authorized **sparse registration/lifecycle observation**: record actual `0x1a9b08` receiver and nonzero member, its constructed table and +8/+0xc targets, with O/E/C identity and thread attribution at the known construction/teardown boundaries. Begin with registration evidence from an ordinary baseline; no candidate replacement is needed. Then source-check the observed targets. Observation of a call/return alone still does not prove all producers settled. If no editor-relevant member exists in that observed path, prefer an explicit owner-controlled service architecture or defer integration; do not schedule another automatic offset-search tranche.

Later original lifecycle tests require independently justified legal hooks/adapter and execution authorization. Use disposable writable fixtures, original loaded-byte identities, sparse stage observations, retained guards, finite independent supervision and ownership-aware cleanup. Choose observation bounds before launch; an observation stop is not clean guest completion. At minimum exercise:

| Case | Deterministic control | Later original evidence / rejection condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One caller-deferred producer | Create ticket before deferral, close G, delay admission, then permit natural execution/result/outcome. Ledger cannot reach zero early. | Attribute an actual caller-held task/box to E; observe unchanged execute/load/result route through final continuation. Reject missing ticket, suppressed original result or drain inferred from queue emptiness. |
| Admission while closed | Race admission with closure; test both serialized outcomes and unknown target classification. | Attempt one matching request while closed; observe refusal before raw capture or proved candidate-held replay. Reject any unaccounted second-stage load/reacquisition. |
| Teardown during settlement | Request teardown with one ticket outstanding; ensure legal handshake, no lock cycle and no early ownership release. | Observe actual O/C/E teardown coordination and all captured-object lifetimes through M callback return. Reject reliance only on E worker-handle clear or stock pointer magic. |
| Delayed/absent acknowledgment | Delay or suppress completion in the model; Keep stays disabled, original unchanged, recovery follows only qualified routes. | Under an authorized controllable boundary, delay M completion. Observe request and later exact local release/ack separately. An absent callback must produce refusal/failed qualification, never fake success or early free. |
| Stale/duplicate acknowledgment | Deliver old G/U after a new generation begins and duplicate an accepted event. | Associate real completion with its retained request context and reader incarnation. Reject advancement by stale callback, pointer reuse, double ticket retirement or reordered original listener behavior. |
| Failure restoration | Fail before closure, during load/result, after local unload and during restoration. | Verify preserved original plus actual legal original reload/player/display and admission reopening, or supported refusal before entry. Reject reopening with old producers outstanding, altered model or permanent busy/teardown obstruction. |

For every pre-commit, Discard, refused, timed-out and failed case, compare the **same selected original** by file identity/bytes and independently decoded PCM, stereo relationship, frame count, sample rate, geometry/timing, supported metadata/attributes, project references and relevant adjunct musical state. Unrelated data must remain intact. A successful listener result, verifier count, static return or black/unchanged display cannot qualify these invariants. Future Keep additionally needs the owner-selected ceiling/tolerance and format envelope, uniform whole-recording gain over both channels, silence unchanged, durable same-original replacement/recovery, preserving reload and fresh PCM/player/display for every owner. A fresh decoder fixture alone cannot discharge those obligations.
