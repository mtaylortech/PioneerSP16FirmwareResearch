**Decision:** retain original firmware/runtime, baseline, sparse attribution, capture burst and tested supervision. Retire repeat initializer, Track and Mixer-navigation discovery. Keep startup-gates paused. Parallel endpoint isolation and RAM-growth causes remain unresolved.

**One next experiment:** does one small channel-1 **Send Amount fader** movement change the original parameter and redraw its value? Map only that control/setter in saved Ghidra; adapt exact gesture matching and sparse before/after parameter receipts. In one fresh guest, perform known MIXER entry, verify stable owned Mixer, then one bounded fader gesture. Require exact consumption, matched handling, original parameter change and corresponding owned pixels. Success establishes editable UI state; absent consumption selects delivery, while handling without parameter/pixel change selects control-state/repaint investigation.

Retain **600s within 1,200s**, independent **605/608/610s TERM/KILL/reap**, endpoint/credential preflight, guards and owned cleanup/postflight. Stop on byte/frame mismatch, fault, guard/supervision failure; uncertain delivery permits no repeat. Previous one-guest/one-tap authorization is consumed; proposed navigation-plus-fader input needs new explicit scope. Nothing is underway.

**Efficiency:** amortize known navigation and the fader test within that same guest; retire navigation hooks after matching. Reuse unchanged validation; add controls only for the new gesture/parameter contract. This avoids repeated initialization; overhead savings were not measured.
