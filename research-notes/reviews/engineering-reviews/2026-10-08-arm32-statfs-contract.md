# ARM32 filesystem ABI and later startup fault — October 8, 2026

The completed 600-second milestone-following attempt found a concrete installed-emulator filesystem ABI defect that strongly explains its later startup failure. This review performs no original guest execution and authorizes no additional attempt. Original firmware, application/libraries, installed runtime and completed evidence remain unchanged.

The guest completed all five original BDFs and 17 faces, both selected enclosing returns and genuine main continuation. It advanced through later original layouts before a synchronous `UC_ERR_MAP` at **490.449 seconds from guest creation**. Captured main PC/r4/r5/r6 were `0x30303030`; SP was `0x7ff3c5b0`, r3 `0x7ff3c4f8`, and libc LR `0x91ee8738`. Host termination after the recorded fault is not guest completion; native final remains missing. Owned closure, direct-child/watchdog reaping and independent postflight passed. Only one original 1×1 InputOnly window and black root captures were established; no input was sent. [Runtime result and exact failures] `../experiments/drawable-input-20261008/evidence-summary.json` (evidence not published), [raw fault events] `../experiments/drawable-input-20261008/fault-events.json` (evidence not published).

The actual installed Qiling `ql_syscall_statfs` and `ql_syscall_fstatfs` each return success after writing `b"0" * 96`. These are ASCII bytes `0x30`, not binary zero. The retained original ARM32 libc `statvfs` pushes 16 bytes of saved registers, reserves 168 bytes of locals, and gives `statfs` an output pointer at local SP+104, leaving **64 bytes** before its saved frame. The installed 96-byte write therefore crosses all four saved words and 16 caller bytes. Its eventual pop predicts r4/r5/r6/PC=`0x30303030` and post-pop SP=`0x7ff3c5b0`, exactly matching the fault. LR resolves to original libc's `__internal_statvfs` immediately after its `memset` call. All source/library identities and relevant original instruction bytes are retained in the [focused static analysis] `../experiments/drawable-input-20261008/boundary-analysis.json` (evidence not published).

The last observed resource caller was `0x3bd474`, followed by access to `/tmp/release.txt`. Original static ordering links its enclosing view through `0x3296b8 → 0x2acb00` to storage initialization, release-file checking and `statvfs("/mnt/emmc/")` at `0x2aee08`. Frame arithmetic independently reproduces both fault SP and r3. These parent transfers and the actual statfs syscall were not hooked in this guest; another release-path reference exists. They remain a strong route inference, not directly observed syscall provenance.

Focused pure Python controls invoked the actual installed handlers, checked ARM mapper 99/100 and actual signatures, and reproduced all 32 excess bytes changing adjacent sentinels. A proposed little-endian `<16I` filesystem fixture emitted exactly 64 bytes and preserved adjacent sentinels. Test fields are synthetic filesystem metadata, not hardware capacity. No ARM instructions, original guest or native syscall dispatcher were executed; installed packages were not modified.
