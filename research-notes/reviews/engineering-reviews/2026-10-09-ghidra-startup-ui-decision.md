# Static startup explained; keep MIXER navigation next

October 9, 2026 — America/Chicago. Review only; no experiment launched.

The latest [Ghidra cycle] `ghidra/HANDOFF.md` (evidence not published) answered what initialization constructs and how its return reaches dispatch. Original instructions establish initializer `0x571d24 → 0x698548`, wrapper success decision → `0xb4060`, then main's nonzero branch → dispatch `0x69afc0`. The new consequential finding is that the 30-iteration status/mode probe can expire and continue at `0x571f58–0x571f60`; initializer completion is not an all-devices-ready invariant. [Original-ELF instructions] `ghidra/exports/critical-original-instructions.txt` (evidence not published) and [81 mapped/6,304 instruction checks] `ghidra/exports/independent-original-verification.json` (evidence not published) support this more strongly than pseudocode. Probe purpose, indirect targets and some types remain inferred.

This static cycle adds no execution, drawing or input evidence. Prior native `bounded_stop` was harness termination, not natural guest completion; closure/postflight passed. Physical boot, DSP/audio and broader usability remain open. Baseline clock defects preclude a speedup claim; analysis counts and tooling duration are supporting measurements. Retire repeat initializer-only, completed resource/completion and identical Track 2 discovery.
