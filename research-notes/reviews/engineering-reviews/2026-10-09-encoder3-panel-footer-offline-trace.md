## Encoder identity and observer registration

At `0x55a1dc/1e0/1f8`, initialization passes E=I+0xd50+0xdc=I+0xe2c and ID3 to `0x55c1cc`; `0x55c1d8` stores ID in E+8. Vector population `0x558c14/1c` puts the same E in index3 of I+0x1c, with append/growth alternatives. The original callback `0x557d1c/20/24` passes that E and signed halfword at copied packet+0x34 to `0x55ca30 → 0x55c354`. The earlier accepted counter-mode/difference map remains unchanged; current mode/history is not known from the Send snapshots.

ViewControllerManager RTTI and constructor establish V's primary vtable `0x8a1c44` (`0x31b224` literal minus 0x90). In initializer `0x320ba0`, r7 retains V and r6 retains A. [Registration call] `../ghidra/encoder3-panel-route-20261009/00320d0c.raw.asm.txt` (evidence not published) `0x320d48` passes A, index3 and V to `0x556364`. That function selects I's encoder vector, invokes E virtual +0x14 and passes V as observer. E's original vtable `0x931584`, slot cell `0x931598`, resolves to [observer insertion] `../ghidra/encoder3-panel-route-20261009/0055bf04.raw.asm.txt` (evidence not published) `0x55bf04`: it rejects null, checks duplicates, grows as needed, stores V in E+0x18's array and updates E+0x20 count. This encoder registration slot +0x14 is distinct from the notification slots on V.

The original notification pairs need careful separation:

| Notification | Exact literal resolution | V target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nonzero raw delta at `0x55c51c` | `0x55c4fc + 8 + 0x480 = 0x55c984 → 0x9315e0 = (0x14,1)` | V primary +0x14 cell `0x8a1c58 → 0x1d1fe4`, original `bx lr`. |
| Conditioned editing at `0x55c4f4`, or additional conditioned notification at `0x55c708` | `0x55c4a0 + 8 + 0x4d8 = 0x55c980 → 0x9315e8 = (0x10,1)`; `0x55c6e8` uses the same literal cell. | V primary +0x10 cell `0x8a1c54 → 0x31a680`. |

Generic invoker `0x55cb10` tests the pair's second word for virtual dispatch and arithmetic-shifts it by one: second word 1 gives zero this adjustment; the first word selects the vtable byte offset. A sufficient original condition is nonzero delta with E+0x58=0: raw notification returns, code branches through `0x55c368/370 → 0x55c498`, rejects zero and issues editing notification with the same delta. Constructor sets E+0x58=0, but that is **not a captured live value**; subsequent conditioning changes it. Full acceleration behavior is not claimed.

The previous review correctly located raw +0x14 but did not resolve the additional editing notification. An intermediate progress update in this trace swapped raw/edit literal labels; direct ARM PC+8 arithmetic corrected it before saving the result. The final distinction above governs this trace. Earlier completed reviews remain checkpoint records.

Active-owner cache assignment `0x31c388` is independently recorded. V+0x58's live content and the loaded active-owner target remain unsampled.

The common original lookup [0x2eb570] `../ghidra/encoder3-panel-route-20261009/002eb570.raw.asm.txt` (evidence not published) uses `[M+0x28]=U`, compares its identifier U+0x84, and on MIXER identifier cell `0x2ebc8c=0xf542ac` returns `[M+0x4c]=C` at `0x2eb624`. The snapshots do not capture U+0x84's current value or M's loaded virtual +0x1c; the common lookup's applicability remains a static reconciliation, not observed callback execution.

Both preserved [snapshots] `../ghidra/encoder3-panel-route-20261009/listener-snapshots.json` (evidence not published) match original guest stdout exactly, at **340.775751s and 347.757197s**, with the same M=`0x18878300`, U=`0x18aafc80`, C=`0x017d1cf0`, F=`0x1a23b740`, W=`0x1a3dad20`, CDM=`0x16b23000`, thread 2000, unit=`0x14aa4700`, parameter=`0x14ad6180`. These are captured relationships, not newly sampled RAM.

Original C secondary vtable `0x89a230` has offset-to-top -8 and shared RTTI with the captured primary vtable. Its +0x10 cell `0x89a240 → 0x2c0044` [thunk] `../ghidra/encoder3-panel-route-20261009/002c0044.raw.asm.txt` (evidence not published) subtracts eight from C+8 and branches to `0x2c004c`. C+8's loaded vtable/slot were not captured; original layout does not substitute for a live receipt.

The panel callback is on a worker; the direct-footer branch depends on thread identity. We do **not** assume the worker satisfies that branch. The accepted earlier input map already identifies the off-owner branch's deferred-post boundary; no deferred consumer/drain/application body is analyzed or reconstructed here. Thus producer/registration and a conditional synchronous footer path are mapped, while a complete executable worker-to-active-footer route remains unproved within this scope.
