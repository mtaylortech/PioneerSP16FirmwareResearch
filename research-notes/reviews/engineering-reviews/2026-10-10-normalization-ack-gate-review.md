# Candidate acknowledgment and load-admission gate review

October 10, 2026, America/Chicago. Step 09e; new 20-minute ceiling beginning 20:33:33. Static inspection only, no implementation. Discovery stopped at 20:37:57 at the first decisive gap. [Final receipt and elapsed time] `../experiments/normalization-ack-gate-review-20261010-step09e/result.json` (evidence not published).

**Decision: the candidate remains unqualified for implementation or file replacement.** The first missing edge is a legal way to settle an already admitted editor load across its two scheduling stages while maintaining reader/listener lifetime. A gate at public editor admission alone misses deferred task re-entry. An unload acknowledgment cannot prove that this upstream producer has finished or cannot reacquire the reader. This sharpens the obligation in the [09d proposal] `../experiments/normalization-ack-gate-review-20261010-step09e/reused/step09d-HANDOFF.md` (evidence not published); it does not show that the proposed requirement to settle admitted work is impossible.

The concrete unresolved ordering is: a public load passes admission and leaves an E task pending; a candidate closes only public admission and obtains an unload acknowledgment from M; the earlier E task subsequently reaches `0xb8570` and submits another load. The code establishes those stages and the wrapper bypass. This is an unexcluded ordering in the current proof, **not an observed execution or a demonstrated stock race**. Single-queue FIFO reasoning cannot settle a producer upstream of that queue. A manager-level gate might cover this producer, but its refusal/completion behavior and reader ownership must first be bound.

The candidate-owned acknowledgment remains a plausible component, conditional on retained reader/listener lifetime and a matching per-operation association. It is insufficient as a complete protocol. No implementation is approved by this review. Discovery did not expand to an exhaustive caller census: other editor entry points, scene reload listeners, the additional `O+0xb0` owner, direct reader-load calls, path aliases, global transport exclusion, preserving reload and fresh PCM/player/display acknowledgments remain open. Existing 09a–09d conditional conclusions are preserved.

The next smallest proposed static question is the legal settlement/lifetime boundary for the captured E load task: bind its argument-0 scheduling/deferred records and any non-destructive way to prevent or finish its later manager submission while E and R remain owned. Require positive evidence for that boundary before expanding producer coverage. If none is found, report that gap rather than introducing flag writes, invented cancellation results, or destructive editor shutdown. This is a proposed next review, not authorization for another tranche, runtime trial or implementation.
