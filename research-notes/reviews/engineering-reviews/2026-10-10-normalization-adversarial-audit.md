## Prioritized findings

### 1. Reconsider the project-save dependency before extending it

**Classification: missing justification / reviewer hypothesis. Priority: high for next-work selection.**

**Challenged claim:** complete stock project-save research is a necessary prerequisite to the requested same-path Keep. The [sequence] `../../PioneerSP16Review/NORMALIZATION-50-SEQUENTIAL-STEPS.md` (evidence not published) orders Steps 04–12 before stock capability in 13 and the numerical specification/oracle in 15–19. The [project-flush handoff] `../experiments/project-flush-20261010-075605/HANDOFF.md` (evidence not published) follows a changed `audioSourceUrl`; [06B](../../history/handoffs/06B-TRACK-ASSIGNMENT.md) follows stock SAVE & REPLACE's named-output reassignment. The feature contract instead demands overwriting the selected original and permits stopped playback.

**Strongest alternative:** if Keep retains the exact file identity/path policy, format, length, rate and timing, the existing project can keep referring to that path. Its reference may require no persistent change. The mapped adjunct fields are `bar`, `bpm`, `sampleNum`, rather than a mapped amplitude field ([06A data binding](../../history/handoffs/06A-AUDIOEDITOR-POSTSAVE.md)). A candidate could potentially preserve the project and complete-name `.dat` byte-for-byte, commit only the audio, and refresh all affected readers. This would still be on-instrument integration, not a renamed-copy substitute. The countercase is an unexamined mandatory dirty/reference/metadata notification or sample-identity rule; those have not been excluded.

**Consequence:** automatic project-save/resume tracing answers stock reassignment consequences, but could unnecessarily widen Keep's destructive write envelope. Good static research is not evidence that its whole route should be reused. The old stock save worker itself rejects an existing destination (`0xb7128–0xb7134`), so that unchanged path cannot implement Keep.

**Smallest correction/discriminator:** explicitly decide whether a length-preserving same-path gain operation must change any project or adjunct field. Require one concrete original owner/notification edge for every asserted additional write; retain unknowns if none is established. Reuse the completed Save trace as a conditional effect map rather than an automatic implementation prerequisite. Do not interrupt Step 08; reassess after its closeout.

### 2. Actual owner refresh is the immediate freshness blocker; another decoder fixture does not close it

**Classification: already documented uncertainty, with a prioritization challenge. Priority: high before integrated Keep.**

**Challenged claim:** the successful release-all fixture, or the README's proposed replacement of its decoder adapter, suffices to advance toward a reliable same-path refresh.

**Exact evidence:** [reader handoff] `../experiments/reader-reopen-20261010-081156/HANDOFF.md` (evidence not published), `0x15247c→0x151548` in its [request export] `../experiments/reader-reopen-20261010-081156/exports/player-process/0015247c.asm.txt` (evidence not published), and [release-all source] `../experiments/reader-cache-fixture-20261010-082651/scripts/release-all.py` (evidence not published). PPM construction allocates sixteen reader slots (`0x14fdac/0x14fdb8`, stride +0x20, loop limit 16). The normal request selects the current scene, with per-scene busy gates. The fixture manually creates exactly two readers and invokes both unloads; it manually invokes player attachment with fabricated scheduler state and replaces the real listener/DSP boundaries.

**Strongest counterexample:** a third real holder, an editor holder, a busy scene or a failed/undelivered unload retains the old cache object. Reload still completes successfully from that object. The decoder is never reached on the original hit branch, so substituting the original decoder cannot solve this ownership problem. No exhaustive actual-owner roster, stopped-state acquisition, all-owner unload acknowledgment or legal restore sequence has been established.

**Consequence:** reject “refresh completed” as a Keep success oracle unless it also identifies fresh decoded bytes and the intended owners. Changing to an uncached filename is also inadequate: it violates same-path Keep and leaves the surviving reader stale in the demonstrated case. This is not a newly discovered stale-audio failure; it is the existing strongest obstacle to extrapolating the fixture.

**Smallest correction/discriminator:** map one complete real stopped-playback refresh transaction: enumerate the affected owners of a shared selected path, legal unload entry and acknowledgments, refusal/delivery conditions, and how each resumes using fresh data. A future disposable real-owner observation should include one surviving-holder negative case and byte/PCM freshness, not just result 0. Original-decoder fidelity remains useful later, but its marginal value for the already-proved cache-hit discriminator is limited.

### 3. Stock writer and host transaction facts must remain separate from a safe Keep design

**Classification: already documented hazards / missing target proof. Priority: high at the commit boundary.**

**Challenged claim:** mapped stock writer success or eight passing host cases permits safe unchanged reuse on the instrument.

**Exact evidence:** writer `0x50a9fc→0x50aa10` deletes destination before move; move wrapper `0x64dc70` contains its own destination deletion before deeper core. Step 07 fixes the old unique-staging wording but leaves collision behavior unresolved. [Host probe] `../experiments/keep-fault-probe-20261010-065129/HANDOFF.md` (evidence not published) records 0644→0600, inode replacement, old-open-descriptor contents, injected boundary errors and no crash/recovery experiment. [06A](../../history/handoffs/06A-AUDIOEDITOR-POSTSAVE.md) also maps sidecar deletion before checked space/open stages with unchecked serialization/flush results.

**Strongest counterexamples:** failure after deleting the original but before move; an extant fixed staging sibling; rename success followed by directory-sync failure; a hard-link alias retaining the previous inode; surviving decoded PCM after namespace replacement. These are distinct problems. Fixed path construction supports a collision hypothesis, **not** a proved overwrite/truncation failure; Step 08 partial results are excluded here.

**Consequence:** stock delete/move wrappers cannot inherit the host model's direct-rename preservation property. Host `COMMITTED_SYNC_OK` means checked calls succeeded in that host environment, not power-loss qualification. Complete confinement fails until aliases/mounts and connected services are bounded; the current symbolic ledger appropriately says so.

**Smallest correction/discriminator:** after the architecture decision, qualify the selected audio transaction and the actual supported storage/reference policy. Reuse host pre-commit versus post-commit-uncertainty outcomes; do not repeat its eight cases as target evidence. Avoid stock post-save sidecar regeneration unless the candidate actually requires it. Close alias/attribute/target sync/recovery obligations for the chosen path before claiming a safe permanent Keep.

### 4. Check whether the exact workflow already exists before making custom work inevitable

**Classification: reviewer hypothesis / missing product evidence. Priority: medium now, high before attachment implementation.**

**Challenged claim:** custom normalization is necessarily needed, or stock inspection can wait until Step 13 without affecting the plan.

**Evidence:** the feature contract explicitly records stock support as unknown; Step 13 depends on completion of Step 12. A narrow [printable-ASCII scan of the held ELF] `normalization-adversarial-audit-20261010/normalization-string-hits.json` (evidence not published) found only an ICU text-normalization import. A case-insensitive `normaliz|normalis` search in the retained manual transcript found no match. Neither is an exhaustive UI/DSP/menu search or proof of feature absence. The contract's stock/custom distinction remains unresolved.

**Strongest alternative:** an undocumented, differently named or version-specific stock workflow could already supply all or part of Normalize/Keep/Discard. Conversely, the named range-save path plainly does not satisfy same-original overwrite. Neither possibility is resolved by deeply mapping project Save.

**Consequence/correction:** advance a bounded stock-capability check and the numerical support/ceiling decision as independent work before committing to an attachment mechanism. Do not declare stock capability absent from the negative string scan, and do not silently choose 0 versus −0.1 dBFS. A reference-sequence change needs the owner's decision; this audit edits no reference plan.
