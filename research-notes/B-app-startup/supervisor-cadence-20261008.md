# Less supervisor housekeeping; firmware speed effect unmeasured — October 8, 2026

Question: can routine supervision cost less while retaining prompt direct-child detection and bounded, ownership-aware shutdown? One sequential **eight-second supervisor-only A/B** used inert helpers; no firmware or display was launched. A2 and the preceding layout-access supervisor sources match. Fresh copies received identical measurement counters. The candidate changes running census/state persistence to 0.5s, keeps the 25ms event loop/direct-child polling, and uses 0.1s census near deadlines and during shutdown. Identity, signaling, PermissionError rechecks and absolute deadlines remain unchanged. [Source match] `latest-a2-source-match.json` (evidence not published), [method comparison] `unchanged-safety-methods.json` (evidence not published), [code identities] `final-code-identities.json` (evidence not published).

| Measurement | Baseline | Candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Process-table queries | 63 | 20 |
| State writes | 261 | 19 |

| Watchdog CPU time | 0.356s | 0.116s |

Queries fell **68%**, writes **93%**. These are supervisor savings, not demonstrated firmware acceleration. Candidate natural-exit detection was 30.3ms; its scheduled TERM delay was 8.6ms. A separate TERM-resistant helper required KILL and was reaped. All four native cases closed/reaped their owned processes and removed private endpoints; postflights passed. Six PermissionError and five census-timeout controls passed. The candidate remains an experimental copy. Owner disappearance and descendant tracking now have a 0.5s routine sampling gap plus query/scheduling time, instead of 0.1s; rapid direct-child polling does not remove that limitation. A [simulated owner-loss control] `controls/owner-loss-results.json` (evidence not published) with 20ms queries detected loss in 85ms/485ms and preserved failure/closure in both conditions; native owner-loss was not tested. [Native deadline/closure timings] `boundary-results.json` (evidence not published) passed. The existing possibility of missing a session-changing orphan between censuses remains. This short comparison does not establish performance under emulator load.

Assessment against the [latest firmware handoff] `../deadline-layout-chain-20261008/HANDOFF.md` (evidence not published):

- **Next technical question:** which constructor encloses resource application at `0x38fb1c`, and does it return? Layout wrapper/getter returns and deadline finalization are already proven. Observe its outer LR/SP, normal pop `0x38fcb4`, and learned caller; drawable UI remains unconfirmed.
- **Repeated initialization:** successful runs spend about 125–134s before font scanning, then 19–22s scanning. A 180s bound leaves only 27–35s after discovery; the already-authorized 295s advisory/300s watchdog bound leaves 142–150s. One observation with sparse related function boundaries amortizes startup better than separate short attempts. No longer bound is needed now.
- **Audio:** earlier successful font-only run had 61,822 calls; A1 had 143,749; current A2 had 55,837. A1's main stayed in libc `fseek` lock acquisition for 31 samples over roughly 151s. Extra worker activity may be a consequence of that wait. A2 changed writable-state provenance and instrumentation, so these totals cannot isolate contention.
- **Scheduling:** current A2 had 815 timed main slices and 1,631 count-mode cache flushes. The retired pixel trial had 15,805 flushes and reached `LS_L_155.csv` about 40s later than font-only. Transitions/callbacks are plausible costs; they were not isolated. Retain the proven font scope and required flushes rather than broadening scheduling on this evidence.
- **Observation:** pixel, allocation/matrix and block hooks are already retired. Keep sparse font/resource returns, main samples and the rare stdio-lock observer. Remaining candidates for a separate instrumentation comparison include per-call audio callbacks, font read/seek callbacks doing host `tell`/`fstat`, startup callbacks constructing suppressed register context, and repeated full five-second summaries. A2 stdout was about 1.82MB.
- **Baseline:** A2 is the best current reference, not a demonstrated repeatable benchmark. Use fresh identical copies of its stated writable seed, fixed hook/fixture identities and the same bound; compare phase times and resource progress, not total audio counts. Reuse unchanged controls; retain changed-code checks, effective command, raw output and automatic cleanup in one note.

Next firmware experiment should keep A2 conditions fixed and identify the outer resource constructor. If worker contention is selected instead, use a same-seed A/B whose sole change is bounded cooperative pacing after verified completed synthetic PCM calls. Record identical phase counters and cumulative time inside `emu_start` by thread in both arms, plus the existing lock observer. Faster main progress with reduced worker share would support contention; a persistent lock wait requires investigation of its owner/wake path. Do not combine audio, instrumentation, fixture and supervisor changes in one causal comparison.
