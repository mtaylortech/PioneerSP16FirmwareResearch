# Step 09a — real holders and the missing legal release window

October 10, 2026, America/Chicago. **Completed static tranche; stopped at a decisive missing edge. Step 09 remains partial and integrated Keep remains blocked.**

Question: for one symbolically selected original audio path in the constructed 1.50 topology, which real owners can retain/reacquire decoded audio, and what legal stop/release protocol excludes reacquisition until commit?

**Result:** AudioEditor has two distinct readers. Its documented `+0x110` reader is constructed by `0x0b9638`; a separate **editor `+0x134` reader is constructed by `0x0bc448 → 0x0bf984`**, the same singleton-cache reader factory as every PPM scene slot. The editor queues load and unload for this second reader with its own listener at editor `+0x100`. Releasing scene readers alone therefore does not establish freshness for a path also loaded by this real editor owner. This is a constructed, conditional static holder, not an observed live third holder.

**First decisive missing edge:** legal editor close `0x0b6518` does not itself establish the `+0x134` unload acknowledgment or exclusion from new load requests. Its body resets ranges and the `+0x110` reader, empties editor `+0x130`, and returns close result 0/9 through a separate notification route. It contains no direct `0x0bd48c` call for `+0x134`. A transitive owner operation or subsequent worker may release it, but that join is missing. Calling close, seeing result 0, or clearing the path is insufficient to declare its shared sample released.

The PPM side independently remains unsuitable as a complete live protocol: normal unload completion clears scene unload-pending then re-enters load; an alternate all-reader acknowledgment branch exists, but the inspected original callers use it during destructive teardown. No non-destructive transport-stop/admission/scheduler-exclusion route spanning every affected owner was bound. Discovery stopped; no guest, fixture or integrated transaction ran.
