Question: can direct replacement preserve explicitly supported host attributes and opaque WAV metadata, and recover truthfully from retained disk artifacts after an owned process is abruptly killed?

[transaction.py] `transaction.py` (evidence not published) extends the old probe's exclusive staging, complete-write loop, validation and direct rename with descriptor-bound namespace operations, pinned source/namespace/lock identities, a participating-writer flock, checked Darwin attributes, immutable versioned intent/ready records, checked close handling, independent commit/sync/refresh dimensions and fresh-process restart recovery. Supported host: macOS14.8.9 / Darwin23.6.0 arm64, APFS `/System/Volumes/Data`, same-device regular single-link files, a private owner-controlled mode0700 transaction directory, and all participating writers using its pinned flock. Preserve ordinary mode, uid/gid where available, exact mtime_ns, binary `com.codex.keep.note` and this host's automatically attached `com.apple.provenance`. Refuse ACLs, flags, special permission bits, unknown xattrs, symlink/hardlink/parent escapes and mismatching selected/namespace/lock identities. Atime is outside preservation; replacement changes inode/ctime/birthtime. Advisory locks do not exclude arbitrary writers; a precommit identity/hash check does not close that race. This is a one-transaction-per-private-namespace development interface, with trusted independently captured configuration, not an arbitrary sample-directory writer.

WAV envelope: RIFF PCM16 mono/stereo, <=1MiB, complete consistent geometry. Compare every opaque supported ancillary chunk and pad byte exactly; success fixture covers LIST/JUNK/cue/smpl and a nonzero odd-chunk pad. Reject PEAK/checksum/unknown metadata, malformed/truncated input, changed geometry or changed metadata. Supplied synthetic PCM doubles small values only to distinguish source/candidate. No normalization math or intended SP16 attribute policy is inferred.
